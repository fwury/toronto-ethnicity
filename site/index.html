Toronto’s Ethnic Origins

2021 Census · Toronto CMA · 1 dot = 5 responses

Basemap
    About this map

    Each dot stands for five responses to the 2021 Census question on ethnic or cultural origin, not five people. People can report several origins, so someone reporting “Irish” and “Italian” appears in both groups, and the dots add up to more than the population.

    Counts come from the Census Profile for each dissemination area (about 400–700 people). Within each area, dots are placed on residential buildings from Statistics Canada’s National Address Register, weighted by each block’s 2021 population and each building’s number of units. A dot’s exact position is illustrative; the count per area is not.

    Limits. Data are a 25% sample, randomly rounded to multiples of 5. Statistics Canada does not publish counts under 10 for a single area, so origins that are thinly spread across the region are under-represented. Only 247 of 488 origins are published at this level; the rest (about 1.2% of responses) are not on the map. Christian, Muslim and Buddhist responses are left out as purely religious. Some new buildings from after 2021 may receive dots.

    Sources: Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population (98-401-X2021006, 98-10-0356-01), 2021 boundary and geographic attribute files, National Address Register (2026). Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.
